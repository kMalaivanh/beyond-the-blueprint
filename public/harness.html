What actually
changed.

Routing a harness in 3D instead of drawing each circuit flat means the wire can be seen going down a wall, around a corner, and holding a minimum bend radius — things a flat drawing cannot represent. Lengths come off the model instead of being worked out by hand, and a change to one model updates the route rather than starting a new sheet.

The manufacturing half of this study is a demonstration of how harness-based installation compares with individual wire pulls. Figures shown are illustrative, drawn from this reconstructed model rather than production data.
